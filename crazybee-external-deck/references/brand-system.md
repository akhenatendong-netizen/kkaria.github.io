# Crazybee brand system

## Core character

Express energetic urban discovery, dining, entertainment, local experiences, and useful product value. Balance confidence and playfulness with enough restraint for commercial and institutional audiences.

## Locked foundations

- Primary language: English.
- Headline font: Poppins.
- Body font: Inter.
- Primary Crazybee red: `#FF3535`.

Use Poppins for all slide and section titles. Use Inter for body copy, captions, labels, footers, tables, and source notes. Use source-matched weights when available; otherwise default to Poppins SemiBold or Bold for titles and Inter Regular or Medium for supporting text.

Format every English cover, section, slide, and card title in strict Title Case: the first letter of every word is uppercase and every remaining letter in that word is lowercase. Avoid sentence case and all-uppercase titles.

## Current visual direction

- Use black or near-black as the dominant cinematic field on hero and product pages.
- Use Crazybee red `#FF3535` as the sharp accent and interaction cue.
- Use white for high-contrast primary type and UI presentation.
- Use nightlife, dining, crowd, venue, and local-experience photography with intentional darkening for text legibility.
- Favor a single dominant composition over dashboards of many cards.
- Allow clean light-background evidence pages when clarity and credibility matter more than atmosphere.

Extract secondary neutrals, overlays, and supporting colors from the relevant approved Figma reference rather than inventing a separate palette.

## Two-mode system

### Immersive mode

Use for covers, section breaks, brand promise, lifestyle context, vision, launch moments, and emotional closing pages.

- Full-bleed or dominant photography.
- Strong dark overlay where needed.
- One short headline.
- Minimal supporting text.
- Crazybee red used selectively.

### Evidence mode

Use for product explanation, metrics, process, market evidence, partnerships, case studies, timelines, and institutional audiences.

- Light or controlled dark background.
- Stable margins and alignment.
- Clear title, one primary argument, and traceable evidence.
- Consistent footer and source treatment.
- More whitespace than immersive mode, without becoming empty or unfinished.

## Non-negotiable identity rules

- Never redraw or distort the logo.
- Never substitute a guessed logo when the approved asset is unavailable.
- Never recolor the logo outside approved variants.
- Never adopt Genwise green-blue colors, logo, or identity.
- Avoid generic consulting-deck card grids, excessive pills, glassmorphism, and decorative charts.
- Avoid random gradients, unrelated stock photography, and inconsistent mockup styles.
