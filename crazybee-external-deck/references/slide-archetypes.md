# Slide archetypes

Select from these families before inventing a new composition.

## Brand and narrative

- Cinematic cover: full-bleed or split photography, centered or anchored logo, one concise promise.
- Section transition: bold phrase, restrained supporting visual, clear change of chapter.
- Point-of-view statement: one argument with strong typographic hierarchy.
- Closing invitation: one next action, contact information, minimal supporting content.

## Product

- Product universe: one hero mockup cluster plus a short product promise.
- Single-flow walkthrough: 3–5 sequential screens with numbered benefit-led captions.
- Feature focus: one dominant screen or device with one benefit and up to three supporting details.
- Before/after journey: user situation, Crazybee interaction, outcome.
- Marketplace view: consumer and merchant sides connected by one clear value exchange.

## Evidence and trust

- Metric proof: one dominant metric and up to three supporting measures with definitions.
- Case study: context, intervention, evidence, outcome; prefer authentic images and current UI.
- Partner proof: curated, approved marks with a statement of relationship; no unexplained logo wall.
- Testimonial: attributable quote, role, organization, date or context where available.
- Process or rollout: simple phases, owners, and outputs; minimize diagrams.
- Institutional page: restrained grid, recurring footer, sources, and careful terminology.

## Selection rules

- Use immersive pages to create attention and evidence pages to earn trust.
- Avoid using the same silhouette on adjacent slides.
- Do not place more than one dominant message on a slide.
- Split a crowded slide before shrinking typography.
