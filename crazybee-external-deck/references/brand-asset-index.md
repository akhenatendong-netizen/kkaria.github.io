## Selection rules

- Prefer the red/gray variants on black, charcoal, or dark photographic backgrounds when contrast is sufficient.
- Use the gray-only variant for restrained secondary branding where the red accent would compete with evidence or UI.
- Use the app icon when the message is specifically about the consumer app or app availability.
- Test contrast on the actual slide background at final size; do not select solely from the filename.
- Keep aspect ratio locked and preserve the full SVG viewBox.
- Do not add shadows, outlines, glows, gradients, or color substitutions unless an approved brand variant explicitly contains them.
- If none of these variants passes contrast, request the correct light/dark brand asset instead of editing the supplied SVG.
