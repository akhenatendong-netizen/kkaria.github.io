# Merchant messaging

Use the approved Crazybee merchant flyer as the source for restaurant-facing positioning. Preserve meaning and claim context; adapt wording to the audience and deck length rather than copying every page.

## Primary promise

- Lead with filling more tables without giving up margins.
- Position Crazybee as a local dining network built to put restaurant owners first.
- Explain that customers pay a small service fee on Crazybee and pay the restaurant in-store through its existing POS workflow.

## Problem contrast

Contrast traditional high-commission platforms with Crazybee only when the task benefits from a competitive frame:

- High commissions versus lower platform cost.
- Delayed payouts versus guests paying in-store.
- Limited ownership versus retaining the full restaurant check.
- Weak repeat value versus driving repeat visits.

Do not name competitors or assert exact comparative rates without an approved source.

## Merchant control

Emphasize that merchants set the rules while Crazybee drives incremental traffic:

- Schedule deals for slower shifts.
- Pause or resume offers.
- Prevent offer stacking.
- Set inventory or redemption caps.
- Choose templates such as spend-and-save, item discount, free dish, promo, and combo offers.

Use current merchant UI frames as product proof rather than illustrating these controls abstractly.

## Payments and operations

Explain the approved workflow clearly:

1. The customer pays a Crazybee service fee.
2. The customer dines in.
3. The customer pays the full deal amount, tax, and tip directly through the restaurant's POS.
4. The merchant retains direct cash flow without a platform settlement delay.

State “no POS integration required” only with the associated workflow explanation: use the existing POS, redeem through a mobile browser, scan a QR code or enter an order code, and ring up the dine-in order normally.

## Credibility proof

Use these proof types from the flyer when relevant:

- Merchant Portal screenshots for Overview, Orders, Marketing, Store, and Finance.
- QR and order-code redemption.
- Deal status, remaining inventory, redeemed count, and usage rate as interface examples.
- Case-study pages for dine-in sales, redemptions, first-time diners, and incremental gross profit.

The flyer contains a named Bloomington restaurant example and figures such as `+72% Dine-In Sales`, `$2,200 → $3,785`, `50+ in 3 days`, and `$872–$1,030` incremental gross profit. Although the flyer is approved for external use, preserve its exact restaurant, period, methodology, and comparison context. Never generalize one case into a company-wide performance claim.

## Merchant narrative sequence

For a merchant pitch, default to:

1. Restaurant pressure and margin tension.
2. Crazybee's merchant-first economic model.
3. How merchants create and control deals.
4. How discovery and redemption work for diners.
5. Why operations remain simple.
6. Real restaurant results with methodology.
7. Setup support and a specific next step.
