RECYCLING LOOP — TASK-LED IMMERSIVE EDITION
16 November 2026

Open Recycling-Loop-Taskflow-English.html for the self-contained version.
For the editable site, serve web/ over localhost or HTTPS. Example:
python3 -m http.server 8799 --bind 127.0.0.1 --directory web

START WITH AN ITEM
Choose an item, check the point rules, prepare it, and optionally log a real action.
After logging, jump straight to local results, explore the material route, or check another item.
The 3D explanation is optional: select any stage, scrub, pause, play a 20-second route, or use 2x speed.
Search, camera/photo confirmation, point QR scan, source records, period comparison, saved activity,
questions, export and undo remain available. The side card scrolls without a visible scrollbar.
Service panels use white surfaces and green actions.

PROTOTYPE LIMITS
Riverside, its rules and all report numbers are fictional. Materials show typical simplified routes.
A logged action is self-reported and is not connected to a tracked item or a processor's record.
Photos stay on the device; item identification needs human confirmation. Camera requires browser
permission and HTTPS or localhost. Camera hardware was not independently tested this iteration.
Questions and activity are browser-local. No operator receives questions; no live data is connected.
A 3D action does not create a recycling log. Reduced-motion and WebGL fallback retain the text tools.
