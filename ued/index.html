About Garden Feelings

A student concept inspired by the University of Bristol Botanic Garden. This is not an official Garden service.

You can leave a feeling directly or explore the optional illustrated islands. No sign-in, name or email is needed.

Choices and optional comments stay in this browser until you remove them or clear site data. They are not sent to the Garden. People sharing this browser may see them.

Decorative balls are not visitor responses. No satisfaction score or visitor count is calculated.
